Privacy Policy – GiftStory

Last updated: October 2026

GiftStory is a private, on-device gift notebook. People, occasions, ideas, memories, photos, coins, and shop unlocks stay on this phone. We do not create an account or upload that notebook.

Data stored on device

  • People, preferences, budgets, and important dates
  • Gift ideas, gifts already given, memory notes, and photos you add
  • Coins, owned shop items, and the active look
  • Purchase tokens needed to restore Remove ads and GiftStory Pro

Notifications

If you leave occasion reminders on, GiftStory schedules a notification on this phone the day before a date. Those reminders are not sent to a server.

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing – coin packs, Remove ads, and GiftStory Pro (handled by Google)
  • Remote config – GET https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N293.json to read the disable field (no personal data is sent)

We do not sell personal data. Contact the developer through the Play Store listing.

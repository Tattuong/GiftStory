## Title

## Short description
Remember what they like, and what you already gave.

## Full description
GiftStory is a personal gift notebook that gets more useful the longer you keep it.

Save what each person likes, the dates that matter, gift ideas, a budget you type in yourself, and the gifts you already gave. Turn an idea into a memory and add a photo. Upcoming birthdays sit on the home page, and this phone can remind you the day before.

Four people are included to start. GiftStory Pro keeps more. Looks, keep-screen-on, and tap sound unlock with coins from the daily reward or 10 coin packs. Remove ads and GiftStory Pro are one-time unlocks.

Everything stays on this device. No gift store and no automatic gift suggestions.

Remote config: https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N293.json — if disable=1, Play purchase sheets stay hidden. The shop still spends coins.
